## Structured uncertainty nodes

Each populated node must have a unique `Uxx` identifier and record:

- phenomenon;
- established knowledge;
- exact unknown;
- relevant explanations;
- relationship among explanations;
- supporting and limiting evidence;
- causal strength;
- scope and confounders;
- why current evidence is insufficient;
- observation type that would reduce uncertainty;
- relevant paper-card references.
