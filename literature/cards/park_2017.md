## Scientific question

Can a robot perform narrow-clearance peg-in-hole assembly with compliant contact behavior without force feedback or a passive remote-compliance mechanism, despite hole-position uncertainty from recognition?

## Exact system and regime

Robot manipulator with eight degrees of freedom and a peg-in-hole apparatus; reported clearance: 0.1 mm. The accessible abstract describes hybrid position/force control and does not identify the manipulator model, peg/hole geometry, vision/recognition system, number of trials, or quantitative success criteria.

## Main claims

### Sensorless, compliance-based assembly strategy

**OBSERVATION:** The authors report experimental verification of their proposed method on an 8-DOF manipulator and a 0.1-mm-clearance peg-in-hole apparatus.

**AUTHOR INTERPRETATION:** Contact-state analysis plus a strategy for recognition-induced position uncertainty, with a controller intended to produce compliant behavior under hybrid position/force control, enables peg-in-hole assembly without force feedback or passive compliance mechanisms.

**INFERENCE:** Within the reported apparatus, physical-compliance behavior may be obtained through the robot/controller arrangement rather than an external force/torque sensor or remote-compliance device. The abstract alone does not establish how robustly this transfers across geometries, clearances, errors, or robots.

**Intervention or measurement:** Proposed contact-state/position-uncertainty strategy and hybrid position/force-control scheme; experimental test on the stated robot and apparatus.

**Observed result:** The abstract states that effectiveness was experimentally verified; no success rate, force trace, baseline comparison, trial count, or uncertainty range is available in the inspected material.
