## Candidates worth bounded pilots

### C001 — U1

- Exact pilot: graded induction-path suppression in one controlled attention-only family across mapping, retrieval-only, prior-matched, and generic next-token tasks.
- Distinguishes: causal induction-path contribution to mapping-sensitive ICL versus retrieval, generic capacity loss, and alternate-path compensation.
- Stop criterion: nonspecific effects, failed intervention engagement, macro-loss-only effects, or failed mapping-state validation.
- Go criterion: reproducible dose-related mapping-specific effects with intact sham, neighboring-path, capacity, and state-assay controls.
- Permitted claim after success: induction-like paths are causally necessary or contributory for the tested mapping-sensitive regime.
- Claim not permitted: induction heads are the universal mechanism of ICL.

### C002 — U3

- Exact pilot: preregistered answer-level mapping contrast across held-out queries and seeds, with calibration, format, verbalizer, and retrieval controls.
- Distinguishes: independent mapping acquisition from priors, formatting, retrieval, and verbalization effects.
- Stop criterion: unstable, absent, or control-explained mapping contrast.
- Go criterion: reliable mapping effect surviving calibrated prior, format, and retrieval matching.
- Permitted claim after success: mapping information contributes independently in the tested regime.
- Claim not permitted: all ICL is mapping acquisition or a unique internal mapping state has been identified.

### C003 — U4

- Exact pilot: transfer and rescue of an extracted task state across held-out arbitrary mappings, with representation-matched shams and deletion controls.
- Distinguishes: functional causal carrier from generic steering, correlation, or prompt-specific geometry.
- Stop criterion: failed transfer, sham-equivalent effects, nonspecific deletion, or dependence on one extraction rule.
- Go criterion: selective counterfactual mapping transfer/rescue with clean-capacity preservation and a prespecified margin over shams.
- Permitted claim after success: a causal intermediate carrier exists in the tested regime.
- Claim not permitted: the carrier is native, unique, localized, or universal.

### C004 — U5

- Exact pilot: validate ICL/IWL evaluators, then compare intact, sham, and selectively suppressed training at matched checkpoints with reciprocal route tests.
- Distinguishes: resource/circuit competition from shared optimization/representation transition and measurement artifacts.
- Stop criterion: evaluator failure, nonlocal intervention effects, or nonreplicated reciprocal effects.
- Go criterion: stable reciprocal, evaluator-robust compensation across seeds with bounded nonspecific training effects.
- Permitted claim after success: competition or shared-transition behavior is supported within the tested synthetic regime.
- Claim not permitted: a universal training law for frontier language models.

### C005 — U6

- Exact pilot: orthogonalize mapping, priors, retrieval, order, and position, then test a prespecified selective state/path rescue across held-out mappings and a second task.
- Distinguishes: a shared causal failure mechanism from heterogeneous, task-specific, or generic intervention effects.
- Stop criterion: absent, nonselective, probe-only, or control-sensitive effects.
- Go criterion: reproducible selective causal effects that transfer across mappings and tasks with matched damage controls.
- Permitted claim after success: a scoped causal contributor to the tested failure mode has been identified.
- Claim not permitted: ICL failures share one mechanism across architectures and tasks.
