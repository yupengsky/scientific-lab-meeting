## Scientific question

When context use fails under order, calibration, long-context, or adversarial perturbations, do the failures arise from estimator instability, positional/circuit competition, or reliance on prior/format shortcuts?

---

## Scientific uncertainty being reduced

The mechanism of conditional ICL failures and whether distinct perturbations reveal a common failure process.

---

## Established evidence

- ICL is sensitive to order, calibration, label space, and prompt format (Lu 2022; Zhao 2021).
- Long-context gains can depend strongly on similarity/retrieval, and restricted models show adversarial context hijacking (Bertsch 2024; Anwar 2024).

---

## Relevant explanations

### H1
Failures reflect instability of the context-computed estimator or task state when examples are noisy, reordered, or contradictory.

### H2
Failures primarily reflect positional or circuit competition that changes which context information reaches the query computation.

### H3
Failures primarily expose shortcut reliance on priors, format, or retrieval rather than a stable task-learning computation.

---

## Relationship among explanations

PARTIALLY COMPETING

Different perturbations may invoke different mechanisms, but matched perturbation signatures can test whether one explanation dominates a regime.

---

## Why current evidence is insufficient

Robustness results depend on perturbation budget, context length, label space, architecture, and metric. Existing findings do not align these variables or measure internal computation during failure.

---

## Potential discriminating observation

Under factorial order, noise, position, retrieval, and semantic-prior controls, compare failure curves with estimator-state probes, positional/path interventions, and mapping-sensitive tests. H1 predicts degradation proportional to estimator inconsistency; H2 predicts position/path-specific effects; H3 predicts persistence under mapping corruption and strong sensitivity to priors or nearest-example availability.

---

## Provisional attack

Use controlled perturbation suites with activation patching and path ablations, matched short/long contexts, and separate mapping, retrieval, and prior probes.

---

## Why now

Existing robustness, long-context, and adversarial-context paradigms can be combined with causal component and intermediate-state measurements.

---

## Scope

Selected transformer LMs on few-shot classification and synthetic sequence tasks across short and long contexts. The conclusions concern tested perturbation regimes, not general safety or all failure modes.

---

## Negative-result information

If no common signature appears, the result would show that “ICL failure” is mechanistically heterogeneous and constrain broad robustness claims. If one signature transfers across perturbations, it would identify a more general failure mechanism.
