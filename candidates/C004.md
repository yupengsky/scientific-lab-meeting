## Scientific question

Which training-distribution and optimization variables cause a model to use in-context computation rather than in-weights solutions, and do observed transitions reflect circuit competition or a shared representation phase change?

---

## Scientific uncertainty being reduced

The causal mechanism behind emergence, persistence, and disappearance of ICL during training.

---

## Established evidence

- Data-distribution manipulations change the utility of ICL and in-weights learning (Chan 2022).
- Training can show transient tradeoffs and circuit formation dynamics (Singh 2023; Olsson 2022; Singh 2024).

---

## Relevant explanations

### H1
ICL and in-weights learning compete for explanatory or representational resources, so changing one solution’s availability shifts mechanism use.

### H2
The transition is primarily a shared optimization/representation phase change, so mechanism timing tracks training dynamics even when solution competition is controlled.

---

## Relationship among explanations

PARTIALLY COMPETING

They predict different responses to interventions that hold data utility fixed while altering circuit availability and optimization conditions.

---

## Why current evidence is insufficient

Current studies confound data utility, training stage, architecture, regularization, and measured ICL. Stable macro performance can conceal a changing mechanism.

---

## Potential discriminating observation

In matched training runs, manipulate data utility and selectively disable or preserve candidate ICL/in-weights paths. H1 predicts reciprocal changes when one solution is made unavailable; H2 predicts transition timing tied mainly to optimization and representation variables, with weaker reciprocal dependence.

---

## Provisional attack

Train controlled transformer families with factorial distribution mixtures, curriculum timing, regularization, and checkpoint interventions; measure behavior, in-weights scores, and causal circuit/state metrics over training.

---

## Why now

Checkpoint-scale analysis and circuit-formation interventions allow mechanism trajectories to be measured rather than inferred from endpoint behavior alone.

---

## Scope

Controlled synthetic sequence and classification training in small-to-medium transformers, with carefully defined ICL and in-weights metrics. It does not establish training laws for all frontier LMs.

---

## Negative-result information

If circuit availability does not shift the balance, competition is weakened and the field gains constraints on interpreting transient tradeoffs. If transitions persist under fixed utility and path availability, a shared optimization/representation explanation remains viable.
